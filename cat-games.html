Free cat games in the browser, compared

Tilcayo Cat Game, Cat Simulator, Kitty Loves Birds, Flip Pounce, Longcat and Yeetcat

Updated September 25, 2026 · by the makers of Tilcayo Cat Game

Six free cat games you can play right now in a browser, on a phone or a computer, with no download. They could hardly be more different: a reflex arcade, a sandbox, a platformer, a one-tap skill game, a 1900-level puzzle and an idle launcher. Here is how they compare and which one fits the kind of game you are in the mood for.

  • Fast rounds and playing against friends: Tilcayo Cat Game — the only one here with online 1v1.
  • Wandering around and causing chaos: Cat Simulator.
  • A classic run-and-jump platformer: Kitty Loves Birds.
  • One-tap levels you can clear in seconds: Flip Pounce.
  • A long, calm puzzle campaign: Longcat.
  • Numbers going up: Yeetcat.

The games, one by one

Tilcayo Cat Game — best for quick rounds and 1v1

You are a tilcayo, a real wild cat from Bolivia, sitting on a moonlit lawn. Mice pop out of holes; every catch adds 0.8 s to a clock that starts at 5 s, and the round ends when it runs out. The lawn grows from 2×2 to 5×5 holes and the mice speed up, so a good run gets frantic. It adds power-ups, a porcupine to avoid, combos, seven hunter ranks, collectible seals, shareable score cards and challenge links. 1v1 online puts two players on the same lawn, best of three. The how-to-play guide has every rule.

Pick it if you want a two-minute game with a score to beat, or a head-to-head with a friend. Skip it if you want levels, a story or a world to explore.

Cat Simulator — best for sandbox chaos

By Critters Studio. You play a mischievous cat exploring cozy rooms, interacting with objects, pulling pranks and knocking things over, collecting stars to unlock new rooms. Keyboard controls (WASD or arrows, Space to jump).

Pick it if you want to roam at your own pace with no timer. Skip it if you want to compete for a score.

Kitty Loves Birds — best classic platformer

By Claudiu V. Guide a black cat through colorful levels to catch every bird, sprinting, jumping and pouncing while avoiding dangerous animals and poison ivy.

Pick it if you like level-by-level platforming. Skip it if you want a quick score-chasing session instead of a campaign.

Flip Pounce — best one-tap skill game

By Finz Games. Tap to aim and launch your cat into the air, flipping and pouncing to land on the goal and rescue a friend.

Pick it if you want short, satisfying levels with one input. Skip it if you want fast reflex play.

Longcat — best puzzle campaign

By Fancade. A cat stretches in a straight line until it hits an obstacle; your job is to fill every empty square without trapping yourself, across more than 1900 levels.

Pick it if you like calm logic puzzles and lots of content. Skip it if you want action.

Yeetcat — best idle upgrade loop

By Bëor Games, on CrazyGames. Each click launches your cat farther; you earn coins, buy speed and power upgrades and chase distance goals. An optional login adds trophies.

Pick it if you enjoy watching numbers climb. Skip it if you want skill to matter more than upgrades.

What makes Tilcayo Cat Game different

  • Online versus: the only game in this list where you can play a live match against another person.
  • A real animal: the hero is Leopardus tilcayo, the first new wild cat species in over 100 years. The about page covers the real cat, with sources and a way to support the refuge that cares for it.
  • Its own site: it lives at tilcayo.cat, not on a game portal, and can be added to the home screen as an app.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free cat game to play in a browser?

It depends on what you want. For quick reflex rounds and 1v1 against friends, Tilcayo Cat Game. For exploring and knocking things over, Cat Simulator. For a classic platformer, Kitty Loves Birds. For a long puzzle campaign, Longcat, with 1900+ levels. For an idle upgrade loop, Yeetcat. For one-tap skill levels, Flip Pounce. All six are free in the browser on phones and computers.

Which cat games can I play against a friend online?

Of the six games compared here, only Tilcayo Cat Game has an online versus mode: two players share one lawn in 60-second rounds, best of three, via quick match or a private room code. The others are single-player.

Which cat game is based on a real cat?

Tilcayo Cat Game stars the tilcayo (Leopardus tilcayo), a real wild cat from Bolivia described in 2026 as the first new wild cat species in more than 100 years. The site includes a sourced profile of the species.

Are these cat games for people or for cats to play?

All six are games for people. “Games for cats” are a different category: apps and videos with moving mice, fish or lasers made for pet cats to paw at on a screen.

Do I need to download anything or create an account?

No. All six run in the browser for free. Yeetcat offers an optional CrazyGames login for trophies, and Longcat also has app-store versions, but neither is required to play.
