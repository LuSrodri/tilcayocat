How to play Tilcayo Cat Game

Rules, power-ups, ranks, seals and tips · updated September 25, 2026

Tap the mice before the clock runs out. You are a tilcayo cat (Leopardus tilcayo) on a moonlit lawn. Mice pop out of holes; every catch buys you time, and the round ends the moment you go 5 seconds without one. It is free, runs in the browser and takes about ten seconds to learn.

The rules in 30 seconds

  • Clock: you start with 5 seconds. Each mouse caught adds 0.8 s (capped at 5 s).
  • Empty slap: tapping a hole with nothing in it costs 0.3 s.
  • Porcupine: it pokes out now and then. Slapping it costs 2 s — leave it alone.
  • Growing lawn: 2×2 holes at the start, 3×3 at 10 s, 4×4 at 35 s and 5×5 at 75 s.
  • Speed: mice get 10% faster at 25 s, 60 s and then every 15 s once the lawn is 5×5.
  • Game over: when the clock hits zero. Your best score is saved on your device.

Power-ups

Power-ups pop out of the holes like mice. Tap one to use it:

Combos

Catches less than 1.5 seconds apart chain into a combo. Each link raises the catch sound a semitone, a draining meter shows how long you have, and callouts fire at 5, 10, 15, 20 and 30. At 10 or more the lawn goes into frenzy. A mouse escaping, an empty slap or a porcupine breaks the chain.

1v1 online

In 1v1 online two players share one 5×5 lawn. Rounds last 60 seconds, the match is best of three and there are no power-ups. The first paw on a mouse wins it, and a porcupine costs 15 points (never below zero). Pick Quick match to face a random player, or Create room and send the five-letter code to a friend. Wins feed the global Top tilcayos ranking.

Challenge a friend

After a round, tap Share my card. The game draws a score card with your mice, rank, time, best combo and seals, and a link like tilcayo.cat/?beat=42. Whoever opens it sees “Beat 42 mice” on the start screen.

Tips for a high score

  1. Don't tap blindly: each empty slap costs 0.3 s, which adds up fast.
  2. Ignore the porcupine. Losing 2 s hurts more than skipping a mouse.
  3. Save your focus for the 5×5 lawn after 75 s — that is where rounds are won and lost.
  4. Grab Freeze when the clock is low; grab Auto-catch when the lawn is crowded.
  5. Keep catches under 1.5 s apart to hold the combo and hit frenzy.
  6. On a computer, press Space to start and use the mouse to catch.

Frequently asked questions

How do you play Tilcayo Cat Game?

Tap the mice as they pop out of the holes in the grass. You start with 5 seconds on the clock; every catch adds 0.8 seconds (up to 5), an empty slap costs 0.3 seconds and a porcupine costs 2 seconds. The lawn grows from 2×2 to 5×5 holes and the mice get faster. The round ends when the clock reaches zero.

What do the power-ups do?

Auto-catch makes the tilcayo catch every mouse automatically for 6 seconds. Full time refills the clock to 5 seconds. Freeze pauses the clock, the mice and new spawns for 4 seconds.

What happens if I hit the porcupine?

In solo mode you lose 2 seconds from the clock and your combo breaks. In 1v1 online you lose 15 points, never going below zero.

How do combos work?

Catches less than 1.5 seconds apart chain into a combo. Callouts appear at 5, 10, 15, 20 and 30, and 10 or more turns on frenzy mode. A mouse escaping, an empty slap or a porcupine breaks the combo.

What are the hunter ranks?

Every solo round ends on a rank by mice caught: Sleepy Kitten (0), Curious Cub (5), Lawn Prowler (12), Night Hunter (20), Shadow Stalker (30), Yungas Legend (45) and Mythic Tilcayo (65).

How does the 1v1 online mode work?

At tilcayo.cat/play two players share one 5×5 lawn for 60-second rounds, best of three, with no power-ups. The first tap on a mouse wins it. You can use quick match against a random player or create a private room and share its code, and wins count toward a global ranking.

How do I challenge a friend?

After a round, tap Share my card. The game draws a score card image and a challenge link like tilcayo.cat/?beat=42; your friend's start screen then shows the score to beat.

Is Tilcayo Cat Game free? Do I need to install anything?

Yes, it is free and runs in any modern web browser on phones, tablets and computers. There is no account and no download; you can optionally add it to your home screen as an app.

Is Tilcayo Cat Game a game for people or for cats?

It is a game for people. You play as the tilcayo cat and tap the mice yourself. It is not a “game for cats” (the videos and apps with moving mice made for pet cats to paw at a screen), although nobody will stop your cat from trying.

What other cat games are like Tilcayo Cat Game?

Other free cat-themed browser games include Cat Simulator (sandbox), Kitty Loves Birds (platformer), Flip Pounce (one-tap skill), Longcat (puzzle) and Yeetcat (idle clicker). Tilcayo Cat Game is the reflex arcade of the group and the only one with online 1v1. See the full comparison of cat games.
