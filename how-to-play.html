How to play Cat The Mouse Company

Rules, power-ups, ranks, seals and tips · updated October 1, 2026

Tap the mice before the clock runs out. You are a cat on a moonlit lawn: a grey tabby at first, and six more cats to unlock in the shop. Mice pop out of holes; every catch buys you time, and the round ends the moment you go 5 seconds without one. It is free, runs in the browser and takes about ten seconds to learn.

The rules in 30 seconds

  • Clock: you start with 5 seconds. Each mouse caught adds 0.8 s (capped at 5 s).
  • Empty slap: tapping a hole with nothing in it costs 0.3 s.
  • Porcupine: it pokes out now and then. Slap it and your cat licks its paw for 2 s, unable to hunt.
  • Green snake: it stays up longer and eats any mouse that pops out right next to it. Slapping it means 3 s of paw-licking.
  • Growing lawn: 4 holes at the start; a new one opens every few seconds, from the middle outwards, until all 25 are dug. The bar at the top shows when the next one comes.
  • Speed: mice speed up smoothly, about 10% every 30 s, and faster once the lawn is full.
  • Yellow lizard: unlock it in the shop and it darts out now and then. It is quick, and counts as 2 mice.
  • Mouse of the day: after every 10 mice, today's dressed-up mouse appears and hops from hole to hole before running off. It is worth 100 points.
  • Game over: when the clock hits zero. Your best score is saved on your device.

Combos

Catches less than 1.5 seconds apart chain into a combo. Each link raises the catch sound a semitone, a draining meter shows how long you have, and callouts fire at 5, 10, 15, 20 and 30. At 10 or more the lawn goes into frenzy. A mouse escaping, an empty slap, a porcupine or a snake breaks the chain.

Daily Challenge: the mouse of the day

Every day has its own fancy mouse, one of 100 hat-and-accessory combinations, and it is the only fancy mouse that shows up that day. Catch 10 of it in a single round to finish the Daily Challenge; fall short and the count starts over next round. Each finished challenge puts that mouse in your album. The list cycles every 100 days, and a full album earns the Mouse Collector seal. After a round, Share copies a spoiler-free result, Wordle-style.

1v1 online

In 1v1 online two players share one 5×5 lawn. Rounds last 60 seconds, the match is best of three and there are no power-ups. The first paw on a mouse wins it, the mouse of the day is worth 3 and the yellow lizard is worth 2 (it joins the match if either player has unlocked it). Each player brings the cat they chose in the shop. A porcupine or a snake doesn't cost points, but the cat that slaps it licks its paw for 2 or 3 seconds while the rival keeps hunting. Pick Quick match to face a random player, or Create room and send the five-letter code to a friend. Wins feed the global Top cats ranking.

Share your hunt

After a round, tap Share for a short result you can paste anywhere: your points, one square per mouse of the day caught (out of 10) and your time. Challenge links like catthemouse.co/?beat=42 still work: whoever opens one sees the score to beat.

Tips for a high score

  1. Don't tap blindly: each empty slap costs 0.3 s, which adds up fast.
  2. Leave the porcupine and the snake alone: licking a paw while the clock runs is the fastest way to lose.
  3. Catch mice next to a snake quickly, before it gulps them.
  4. Watch the bar at the top: a wiggling mound shows where the next hole will open.
  5. Grab Freeze when the clock is low; grab Auto-catch when the lawn is crowded.
  6. Keep catches under 1.5 s apart to hold the combo and hit frenzy.
  7. On a computer, press Space to start and use the mouse to catch.

Frequently asked questions

How do you play Cat The Mouse Company?

Tap the mice as they pop out of the holes in the grass. You start with 5 seconds on the clock; every catch adds 0.8 seconds (up to 5) and an empty slap costs 0.3 seconds. The lawn starts with 4 holes and a new one opens every few seconds until all 25 are dug, while the mice slowly speed up. The round ends when the clock reaches zero.

What do the power-ups do?

Auto-catch makes your cat catch every mouse automatically for 6 seconds. Full time refills the clock to 5 seconds. Freeze stops the clock and the whole lawn for 4 seconds: every critter freezes in a block of ice, mid-move.

What happens if I hit the porcupine?

Your cat stops to lick its sore paw and can't catch anything for a moment: 2 seconds after a porcupine, 3 after a snake. Your combo breaks too. The same goes for 1v1 online.

How do combos work?

Catches less than 1.5 seconds apart chain into a combo. Callouts appear at 5, 10, 15, 20 and 30, and 10 or more turns on frenzy mode. A mouse escaping, an empty slap, a porcupine or a snake breaks the combo.

What are the hunter ranks?

Every solo round ends on a rank by mice caught: Sleepy Kitten (0), Curious Cub (5), Lawn Prowler (12), Night Hunter (20), Shadow Stalker (30), Lawn Legend (45) and Mythic Mouser (65).

How does the shop work? Which cats can I unlock?

Every round adds the points you scored to your wallet (in 1v1, 10 points for each mouse you caught). Spend them in the Shop on the start screen: you start with the Grey Tabby, and can unlock the Orange Tabby (150,000), Badger (400,000), Black Cat (800,000), White Cat (1,400,000), Calico (2,200,000) and the Tilcayo (4,000,000), the rare wild cat from Bolivia. The Yellow Lizard (600,000) is a new critter to hunt: once unlocked it darts out now and then and counts as 2 mice. Your cat and your lizard also come with you to 1v1 online.

Are there rewards for playing every day?

Yes. Days played in a row count as a streak, and four seals reward it: Regular (5 days in a row), Loyal Hunter (15), Lawn Keeper (60) and Company Legend (90). A round of solo or a 1v1 match both count for the day.

How does the 1v1 online mode work?

At catthemouse.co/play two players share one 5×5 lawn for 60-second rounds, best of three, with no power-ups. The first tap on a mouse wins it, the mouse of the day is worth 3 and the yellow lizard (if either player unlocked it) is worth 2. Each player brings the cat they chose in the shop. You can use quick match against a random player or create a private room and share its code, and wins count toward a global ranking.

How do I challenge a friend?

After a round, tap Share my card. The game draws a score card image and a challenge link like catthemouse.co/?beat=42; your friend's start screen then shows the score to beat.

Is Cat The Mouse Company free? Do I need to install anything?

Yes, it is free and runs in any modern web browser on phones, tablets and computers. There is no account and no download; you can optionally add it to your home screen as an app.

Is Cat The Mouse Company a game for people or for cats?

It is a game for people. You play as a cat and tap the mice yourself. It is not a “game for cats” (the videos and apps with moving mice made for pet cats to paw at a screen), although nobody will stop your cat from trying.

What other cat games are like Cat The Mouse Company?

Other free cat-themed browser games include Cat Simulator (sandbox), Kitty Loves Birds (platformer), Flip Pounce (one-tap skill), Longcat (puzzle) and Yeetcat (idle clicker). Cat The Mouse Company is the reflex arcade of the group and the only one with online 1v1. See the full comparison of cat games.
